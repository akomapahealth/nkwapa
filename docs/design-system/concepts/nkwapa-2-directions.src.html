Epic #229 · Nkwapa 2.0 redesign

Three directions for one clinical workspace

Each direction keeps Nkwapa's teal and gold, Source Serif 4 and IBM Plex Sans, and every permission, field and offline behaviour. They differ in how the product is organised on screen. All three are drawn on the same surfaces so you can compare them directly: a manager's Today workspace, a volunteer at a station on a phone, and the public landing hero. Use the theme switch above to see each in dark mode.

What I need from you

  1. Approve one direction, or name what to change.
  2. Confirm the two navigation moves in epic #229: Message delivery to the header, Clinic settings to the profile menu.

Recommendation: A, Ruled Chart. It fits laptop-based stations and dense review work, carries every role from volunteer to system admin, and builds on the component contract that already ships. Reasons are under Compare.

What the baseline shows

Captured today on seeded E2E data: 21 routes, five roles, three widths, light and dark (126 screenshots), with axe run on each at desktop and mobile (84 scans). The product is accessible and stable. Its problem is hierarchy.

7,342px

The staff dashboard is about eight screens tall at 1440px wide. Work that needs someone sits below clinic-wide averages and ten charts.

5th section

A volunteer's own work comes after clinical averages and a smoking-status chart on mobile, roughly 1,500px down.

1critical axe issue

A button with no accessible name on New Patient, light and dark. Everything else passed WCAG 2.2 AA. No route scrolls sideways at 390px.

3role badges

The header shows every role as a gold pill, next to a clinic switcher, a sync chip, a refresh button and the page's own Refresh. Gold reads as brand, so roles look decorative.

21nav items

One long sidebar for every role, with Notifications and Clinic settings mixed into Oversight. In icon mode every item sits in its own white tile, and the mint panel stops short of long pages.

Rawenums

Charts and tables print system values (IN_REVIEW, DRAFT, UNKNOWN, FINALIZED), and smoothed lines draw values between days that never happened.

Full findings, the route inventory (55 routes, 11 more than the epic lists) and the role-task matrix are in docs/design-system/PHASE-00-AUDIT.md. All patient names on this page are synthetic.

Ruled Chart

Every screen reads like a well-kept observation chart: ruled, time-ordered, numbers first. Chrome drops to hairlines so readings, waits and decisions carry the hierarchy.

Kept from the ticket wallet: nothing disappears, it cancels. A corrected reading stays visible, struck through, with who changed it and when.
Kept from the dance score: length means time. On the station line, a bar's length is how long the patient has waited.
Kept from the data field: numerals lead. The figure is set first and large, its label second and quiet.
Kept from the consumer app: teal only on what can be pressed or what is selected. Status keeps its own colours.
Shell
Option A. A labelled 232px sidebar on the canvas, collapsing to 64px icons with tooltips. Groups follow app-nav.ts.
Header
Clinic switcher with the active role as text, sync state, Message delivery, account. One refresh, not three.
Surfaces
Hairline-ruled groups on the canvas. Cards only for things that float: dialogs, sheets, popovers.
Type
Source Serif 4 for page titles and a patient's name. Plex Sans with tabular figures for everything else.
Charts
Straight segments between real readings, direct labels, a table toggle. No smoothing.
Motion
Hovering a lane dims the others (150ms opacity). Nothing else moves.

Risk: restraint can read as plain. The hierarchy depends on type, rules and spacing being exact, so it needs a strict polish pass.

The Folder

The visit is an object that travels, like the OPD folder a patient carries from table to table. Each station stamps it; the doctor opens the whole folder. Nothing is filed until it is stamped.

Where it comes from: the paper folder and folder number every Ghanaian outpatient clinic runs on, and the stations-as-tables layout of an Akomapa screening day.
Signature: stamps. Done is a solid stamp with time and initials, not yet is dashed, saved offline is orange "Held".
Shell
Option B. A 76px labelled rail for the five most-used areas plus More, and a contextual panel that appears only inside Today, Patients and Queues.
Header
Breadcrumb of where you are, clinic, sync, Message delivery, account.
Surfaces
Folders are the one card type. The open folder takes the pale gold accent, the only gold on screen.
Type
Serif for the patient's name on a folder and for page titles. Plex for stamps and fields.
Mobile
Bottom bar with four places. The folder fills the screen with station tabs.
Motion
A folder opens from its tab (200ms, ease-out). Stamps appear without animation.

Risk: the metaphor fits stations, patients and review well and strains on admin, audit and analytics, which have no folder. Card density also grows quickly on a busy day.

Margin Review

A page of record with a working margin. The record runs in the centre, a rail of station keys holds it together, and the wide margin carries what needs a decision, bracketed to the line it is about.

Where it comes from: the reference-setting challenger (two columns around a key rail, apparatus in the margin), fused with how doctors annotate a chart.
Signature: margin notes. Review, cosign and follow-up sit next to the evidence instead of on another screen.
Shell
No sidebar. A top bar with four grouped menus, and a page index in the left margin.
Header
Clinic, sync, Message delivery and account sit at the right of the same bar.
Surfaces
No cards. Hairlines, a 1px ink rule under the bar, square corners (2px), teal as the ruling colour.
Type
Serif titles at a larger size; small caps for margin headings.
Mobile
The key rail becomes a strip of station keys across the top.
Motion
A margin note's bracket draws to its row on focus. That is all.

Risk: 21 destinations in four menus are one click further away. Square corners break the radius scale in MASTER.md, and three columns need 1280px or more, so laptops at 1024 to 1366px lose the margin.

How they compare

Scored against the epic's acceptance criteria and what we know about the clinics: shared laptops at stations, phones in the portal, unreliable Wi-Fi, five staff roles.

Criterion A. Ruled Chart B. The Folder C. Margin Review
Station throughput on a laptop Next patient and wait time are the first things read. Folder-per-patient matches the room exactly. Clear, but the margin is lost below 1280px.
Doctor review and sign-off Ruled readings with struck-through corrections. Open the folder, every station stamped. Its best case: notes beside the evidence.
Admin, audit and analytics Tables and charts are its native form. No folder to show, falls back to plain tables. Works, but menus hide 12 oversight and admin pages.
Fits the shipped component contract Extends AppMetricGroup, DataTable and the five states. Needs a new folder and stamp family. Changes the radius scale and replaces the sidebar.
Navigation for 21 permission-filtered items Labels always visible, collapsible. Five on the rail, the rest under More. Four menus, one click deeper.
Distinctiveness Quiet, recognisable by its rules and figures. The most ownable for Akomapa. Strongly editorial.
Landing page reach A real trace, honest and specific. Photo plus folder tells the story at a glance. Elegant, text-heavy above the fold.
Build risk across phases 01 to 09 Lowest. Mostly hierarchy, tokens and layout. Medium. New card family, rail and More menu. Highest. New shell, new radius scale, menu IA.

Challengers that were weighed and not built

Declined

Airline ticket wallet

Strong on stations, foreign to a Ghanaian clinic, and one leg per sheet hides the rest of the visit from the doctor. A kept its "cancel, never delete" rule.

Declined

Dance score

Time flowing upward is beautiful and unreadable at a station. A kept "bar length is wait time".

Declined

Black-and-white data field

Conflicts with the brand colours and strobes. A kept "numbers before labels".

Declined

Streaming poster wall

Patients are not posters. A kept the dimming of everything except the row you are reading.

Declined

Warm consumer app

Right for the patient portal, too loose for staff. A kept "teal only on what can be pressed"; Phase 06 can borrow more of it.

Always available

The category standard

A shadcn collapsible sidebar, KPI cards and charts, polished to the level of Heidi's app. Safe and familiar. Ask for it by name if you prefer it.

Recommended: A, Ruled Chart

  • It puts "what needs me now" first for all five staff roles with data the API already returns. Nothing in A needs a new endpoint.
  • Stations run on shared laptops, so a labelled sidebar and dense ruled tables suit the work better than a phone-first rail.
  • It extends what ships today (hairline metric groups, DataTable, the five states, the help affordances) instead of replacing it, so phases 01 to 05 stay small and reviewable.
  • It is the only direction that is as strong on admin, audit and analytics as on clinical screens.
  • If you want more identity, B's stamps can become A's status language for visit progress without adopting the folder shell. I would treat that as a change to A, decided now.

Before Phase 01 starts

  1. Pick A, B or C, or ask for the category standard.
  2. Message delivery as a header icon (I used a send icon, not a bell, so it does not read as an inbox) and Clinic settings in the profile menu: confirm or change.
  3. Manager and Director have no E2E identities yet, so their columns of the QA matrix cannot be captured. Approve adding them to the seed (a db change, outside this epic's guardrail) or accept the staff identity as a stand-in.
