# R7.5 review: Error and empty states

**Scope:** frontend-only on `ui-refresh/r1-1-tokens`: error and empty states across the workspace,
portal and public pages, against MASTER section 8 and product principle 2 (never blank the screen;
an empty state is never shown when the real problem is the network). The owner also asked for
per-person avatar colours. Clinical save semantics, validation, permissions, flags, sync and Keycloak
are unchanged. Local commit only; no push. No separate agent review ran.

Guidance applied: Impeccable (context loader, harden, clarify and polish playbooks, craft floor,
detector over the changed files: no findings, regex pass only), Emil Kowalski's design engineering
(state swaps happen tens of times a day, so they stay instant: no new motion), Apple's design
principles (Responsibility: an attestation of "none" must never be offered against a list nobody
could see; Feedback: errors name the problem and the recovery). Mode: Operate (workspace, portal).

## How the findings were made

An inventory of every `ResourceState`, `EmptyState`, `EmptyStateCard`, chart-section state and
hand-rolled "No …" branch across `app/` and `components/` (about 50 files), reading each empty
branch's guard. Most routes were already correct: issue #22 put list pages on `ResourceState`, and
the ops pages guard `error && rows.length === 0 ? null`. The defects were in hand-rolled chart
panels, the chat picker and the whole-page screens.

### Avatar tones

Hues 188, 208, 230, 255, 280, 305, 330 and 50, chosen to stay clear of red (error), green (success
and the online ring) and amber (warning), so a tone never reads as a status. Light: fill `h 60% 88%`,
ink `h 60% 25%` (gold 75%). Dark: fill `h 33% 26%`, ink `h 55% 86%` (gold 42/70%). Initials measure
**5.45:1 to 9.70:1** in light and **6.29:1 to 7.89:1** in dark. The colour is decoration on
`aria-hidden` initials; the name is always beside it. Deterministic, not random: a colour that
changed per render or reload would be noise. Seeded by display name (email for an unnamed invite),
because that is what every avatar site has; a renamed person changes colour (noted in the helper).
A cancelled or expired invitation stays grey.

## Checked and left alone

- List routes on `ResourceState` (patients, queues, volunteers, screening days, research, reports,
  duplicates, portal): offline-blocked, initial error and empty are already distinct.
- Ops pages (today, stations, my assigned, appointments, audit, notifications, data requests,
  admin users): empty branches already guard on error.
- Failed clinical saves at stations (glucose, eye, counselling, diabetes, prescriptions) are inline;
  none relies on a toast.
- Motion: no state transition was added. Skeleton-to-content and error-to-retry swaps stay instant
  (MASTER 9.1 rule 10).

## Ranked findings left open

- **P2.** "Record no known medications" stays available offline against an empty device copy. The
  offline outbox for reconciliation is deliberate and its semantics are out of scope; the empty
  state now says the device has no copy. Needs a product decision.
- **P3.** Audit and notifications still say "0 events loaded" above a failed first load.
- **P3.** `global-error.tsx` replaces the root layout, so the app's fonts and tokens may not load
  there; pre-existing, not verified.
- **Not checked:** screen readers, real devices, real Safari.
