# R7.1 review: Motion review

**Scope:** frontend-only on `ui-refresh/r1-1-tokens`: every animation and transition in the
workspace, portal and public pages, against MASTER section 9.1 and R0-AUDIT section 6. Plus the
page eyebrow decision carried from R6. Local commit only; no push. No separate agent review ran.

Guidance applied: Impeccable (context loader, craft floor, detector over the changed files: no
findings), Emil Kowalski's design engineering (frequency test, enter/exit asymmetry, keyboard
rule), Apple's fluid-interface guidance (spatial consistency, interruptibility, reduced motion as a
cross-fade). Mode: Operate (workspace, portal), Persuade (landing).

No `transition: all` (`transition-all`) and no bare `transition` utility anywhere in `app/` or
`components/`. No `ease-in` on an entrance; the landing deck's `power2.in` is on cards leaving
during a scrub. Nothing starts from `scale(0)`; the smallest start is the R6.1 showcase marker at
0.85. Hover motion: `tailwind.config.js` sets `future.hoverOnlyWhenSupported`, so every `hover:`
and `group-hover:` (including the arrow nudges in the release banner, clinic settings and portal)
already compiles inside `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`.

## Page eyebrows: retired

The craft floor bans a kicker above a heading, and every one in the workspace either repeated the
navigation ("OVERSIGHT" over Metrics, "YOUR ACCOUNT" over Profile, "CLINIC SNAPSHOT" over the
clinic's name) or the obvious ("SECURE SIGN IN" over "Sign in to Nkwapa"). Removed from 20 page
headers and the sign-in page. Where the line states a fact the title cannot carry, it stays as a
sentence-case muted line: the station on a station visit, the active clinic on research exports
(`AppPageHeader` `context`, renamed from `eyebrow`), "New visit" and "Encounter" above a patient's
name, "Step 2 of 3" in onboarding, "Day schedule" or "Week schedule" on appointments. Field
labels, legends, table headers and the full-screen status badges keep `.text-eyebrow`: they label
content rather than introduce it. Recorded in MASTER section 4.4.

## Ranked findings left open

- **P2.** Removing the sheet's `transition-transform` (nothing transitions transform; the keyframes
  own it) made every handle-drag test fail in Chromium: the sheet stopped following the pointer.
  Restoring the class fixed it, confirmed by bisecting. Not root-caused; the class stays with a
  comment.
- **P2.** Dialog consumers that set their own durations or keyframes outside `DialogContent`
  (the welcome tour uses Radix directly) do not get the keyboard-instant rule; the tour opens on
  its own, not from a key, so it was left.
- **P3.** Dialogs still use keyframes (tailwindcss-animate), so a dialog closed mid-entrance
  restarts its exit from the end state instead of reversing. Menus and Selects are transitions.
  Dialogs are occasional and centred, so the cost was not worth a second presence clock.
- **P3.** The welcome tour's step slide (220 ms) is a keyframe-free Motion transition with a
  fixed duration rather than a spring; it plays once per person.
