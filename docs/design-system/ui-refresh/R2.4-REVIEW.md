# R2.4 draggable Sheet review

R2.4 implements the shared Sheet gesture unit of the Layered Clinical Workspace
refresh. Production changes are confined to `components/ui/sheet.tsx` and the new
`components/ui/use-sheet-drag.ts`. No dependency, clinical save, queue action,
provider, API, schema, permission or route destination changed.

## Reference and interaction decisions

The live Claude artifact and embedded claudeusercontent frame were inspected:
https://claude.ai/artifact/ESPAMmNxLt1caa2owyjSX5 . The checked-in
`r0-pilot/index.html` supplies the matching visual authority. Existing R2.3
synthetic evidence is available locally as the before baseline. No real-patient
or third-party reference directories were inspected.

All six actual Sheet call sites were read: staff/portal Header left navigation,
public right navigation, staff More at the bottom, right sync center, right
patient comparison and right staff-access details. Each keeps its existing side
and presentation. The shared primitive supports all four configured edges,
including top, which currently has no production consumer. The portal Header's
left Sheet is controlled by default closed props because PortalLayout does not
supply mobile-open state. That existing portal-shell integration belongs to R2.5;
its left-edge interaction is exercised through the production primitive fixture.
A patient without sync permissions or held/pending work retains no sync pill.

A separate handle, 64 by 44px at default text size, supports dragging and
click/keyboard close.
It occupies a nonshrinking row above the existing content and has visible focus
and an accessible directional label. The close X remains. Only the handle
captures a primary pointer and disables native panning/selection. Sheet bodies
keep normal scrolling, selection, links and form controls. Secondary pointers
cannot take over a gesture. No body-wide swipe competes with scrolling or clinical
inputs.

While held, CSS `translate` follows the pointer directly, independent of the
existing transform entrance/exit animation and without tweening. Pulling towards
the attached edge meets increasing resistance. Release dismisses after 30% of the
Sheet dimension, capped at 160px, or an outward flick above 0.5px/ms after at least
24px. Releasing inward restores the Sheet. A short release sample expires after
100ms of inactivity.

Restoration uses the installed Motion physical spring. Apple's sheet response
0.3s and damping ratio 0.8 are mapped to mass/stiffness/damping and carry release
velocity. Inspection of the installed `motion-dom` implementation confirmed that
duration/bounce springs discard velocity, so that initial configuration was
replaced. Regrabbing stops the spring at its current presentation value. An active
entrance is frozen at its current translation before gesture ownership begins.
Reduced motion retains direct user manipulation and removes the release spring;
the existing global entrance/exit clamp remains.

Cancellation/lost capture restore without dismissal. Resize, visual-viewport
resize, window blur and page hiding reset displacement and release capture.
History and pathname changes close via the existing Radix close control. External
closes stop a held gesture/spring; unmount cleans up listeners, observer, capture
and animation. A trailing click after a moved gesture is suppressed, including
a drag interrupted by resize. Movement across either axis counts towards click
suppression, so a cross-axis drag cannot turn into a close click.

Radix continues to own dialog roles, title/description, Escape, outside dismissal
and focus containment. Custom open/close autofocus handlers are composed and
retain precedence. More-to-sync remains sequential. Default focus return remembers
the actual opening control and falls back to main if it is gone, hidden or body.
Selection-controlled comparison/details panels have no Radix Trigger. The roster
also replaces its opening cell button during selection updates, before autofocus;
the consumer check verifies the original node is disconnected and requires main
focus in that case. No modal/focus-trap override or axe exclusion was introduced.

## Validation

The final-source focused browser selection passed **77/77 cases**, comprising six
authentication setups and **71 distinct browser checks**. This is a single green
run after the focus and cross-axis fixes, with the final audit-flag build.

The final audit-flag production build, standalone TypeScript, changed-file ESLint,
Prettier, whitespace check and detector passed. ESLint retains the existing
repository-root Pages-directory configuration notice while exiting successfully.
Jest with two workers: **93 suites / 1019 tests passed**. The sandboxed build was
interrupted after stalling at compilation; outside-sandbox builds succeeded.
No flag-off browser build or full e2e suite is claimed.

The fifteen dedicated Sheet cases exercise all four edges in both themes;
320/390/599/600/744/767/768/1024/1199/1200/1440 for bottom/right presentations;
44px target floors; direct tracking; rubber band; reversal; cancellation and lost
capture; regrabbing a moving spring; resize reset; Escape while held; repeated
opening; browser history; reduced motion; CDP touch ownership and secondary-pointer
exclusion; short outward flick; and 12px/90px cross-axis movement. Long bodies
scroll to their final action at all eleven widths in both themes and at 200% root
text on the 320px bottom presentation. Draft text remains intact through a
cancelled drag. Four open-fixture full-page axe assertions are clean, as are the
two actual open-More scans retained as JSON.

The ten actual-consumer cases retain More-to-sync sequencing and entry-point
focus, drag dismissal of More/sync/comparison/staff access/public navigation,
comparison write protection, replaced-trigger main focus and unchanged patient
portal eligibility/navigation. Existing focused coverage also passes Header/sync
states, clinical recovery controls, public menu navigation and keyboard dismissal,
one mounted patient/invitation table state, actual sync conflict recovery and
an offline edit replayed once. The dormant left portal menu and unused top side
are not claimed as live consumer flows.

The six-role normal-route audit passed **12/12 tests**, comprising six fresh
authentication setups and six role sweeps. Its **228 route/theme/width rows** have
**zero recorded errors, zero axe violations including minor findings, and zero
horizontal overflow**. These normal-state scans do not clear earlier open-overlay
findings.

**Evidence:** **193 synthetic PNGs** under ignored `evidence/r2-4/after/`: 114
normal-route light-theme images, 54 Sheet fixture/actual-consumer images and 25
shell images. The Sheet and shell supplements include both themes. Two clean
open-More axe JSON files are in the shell supplement. `route-audit.json`,
`validation-summary.json` and `diagnostics/` are beside `after/`; diagnostics retain
candidate/final logs and the empty detector findings JSON. The 232 R2.3 PNGs are
available as the before baseline. Evidence remains untracked and was not
force-added. Screenshot inspection covered synthetic bottom/right fixtures and
actual More, sync, staff access and public navigation; no physical gesture feel
is inferred from those static images.
