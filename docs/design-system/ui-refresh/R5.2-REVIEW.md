# R5.2 review: Authentication screens

**Scope:** frontend-only on `ui-refresh/r1-1-tokens`: `/login`, the `OnboardingFrame` steps
(`/accept-invite`, `/claim-record`, required `/account/name`), the Keycloak login theme, `/account`
and the non-required `/account/name`, plus the R5.1 follow-ups the owed critique found. Local commit
only; no push. No separate agent review ran.

Guidance applied: Impeccable (context loader, detector over every changed file and the R5.1 portal
screens: `[]`, plus a critique pass on the portal forms), and the R4 contracts. Emil and Apple
skills were not loaded: no motion was added, and the existing sign-in motion (rise-in, photo
reveal) was left as #277 specified it.

## What changed

- **One sheet.** `/login`, every `OnboardingFrame` step and the Keycloak pages sit on the same
  inset white sheet as the portal and staff shells: full bleed under 600px, then 8px inside the
  canvas with a 12px radius and a hairline. This closes the R1.1 "login on the canvas" gap.
- **Audience control.** "I am signing in as" keeps its native radio group but takes the pilot
  `.seg` look (surface-2 track, raised card knob, primary label) instead of a filled teal segment,
  so it matches `SegmentedControl`. 42px segments in a 3px track (48px), 48px segments on touch.
- **Keycloak fields.** Sign-in username was `autocomplete="off"`, which blocked password managers
  on the one screen everyone uses daily; it and reset-password now say `username` (plus
  `autocapitalize="none"`, `spellcheck="false"`). Errors on sign-in, reset and choose-password are
  tied to their fields with `aria-describedby`. "Forgot password?" and "Back to sign in" measured
  18 to 20px tall on a phone; they are 44px under a coarse pointer.
- **Account.** `/account` cards became ruled `RecordSection`s with muted 16px icons; "Your access"
  is hairline rows; the phone form and the shared `StaffNameForm` end in `FormActions` (this also
  reaches the admin user sheet and the onboarding name step). The non-required `/account/name` lost
  its shadowed `rounded-xl` card for `AppPageHeader`; Cancel is an outline button. The staff and
  patient permission split is unchanged.
- **Claim record.** Actions sit in `FormActions` (Refresh invitations, then Claim patient record
  last); date of birth has `autocomplete="bday"`.
- **R5.1 follow-ups (owed critique).** The appointment-request reason suggestions were `rounded-full`
  pills (R1.3 removed those) and gave no sign of which was chosen; they are 4px chips with
  `aria-pressed` and the selected look. The portal BP, diastolic and pulse fields ask for the numeric
  keypad (`inputMode="numeric"`).

Unchanged: Keycloak security, flows, messages and required actions; every redirect, route and
permission; every e2e hook (`#username`, `#password`, `#kc-login`, `I am signing in as`, `Save
phone`, `Your access`, `Your name`).

## Decisions

- Keycloak controls stay 48px on every pointer. The app's 40px mouse field would clip the 44px
  password toggle that sits inside it, and the theme has no shared token to switch.
- The Keycloak theme stays light only (#83).
- The dashed "Shows on records as" preview in `StaffNameForm` stays: it is live feedback, not a
  container.

## Ranked findings left open

- **P2.** `OnboardingFrame` on the sheet was exercised by `patient-claim` and `staff-invite`, but
  the evidence spec could not screenshot it: the seeded patient and staff have already claimed or
  accepted, so both routes redirect. `patient-account-setup` was not run (it fails on release/dev).
- **P3.** On `/account` the work phone form shows "Remove phone" (disabled) when no phone is saved.
  That is existing behaviour, now more visible on its own `FormActions` row.
- **P3.** In the 1440 full-page shot of staff `/account`, the fixed chat button sits over the end of
  the phone `FormActions` row. Where it lands depends on scroll; the button was not overlap-tested.
  The chat button is not new.
