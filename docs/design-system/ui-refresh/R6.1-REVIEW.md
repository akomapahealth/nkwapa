# R6.1 review: Public site

**Scope:** frontend-only on `ui-refresh/r1-1-tokens`: the landing page, `/privacy`, `/terms`,
`/cookies`, `components/landing/*` and the product screenshots in `public/`. Local commit only; no
push. No separate agent review ran.

Guidance applied: Impeccable (context loader, craft floor, critique on rendered evidence, detector
over `components/landing` and `app/(marketing)`: `[]`), Emil Kowalski's design engineering and
Apple's fluid-interface guidance for the motion pass. Mode: Persuade (landing), Read (legal).

## What changed

- **Product screenshots replaced.** `public/desktop.png` still showed the pre-refresh UI (gold role
  tags, the old dashboard) for "Akomapa Clinic - UCC" with 68 patients and 68 drafts. That count
  matches the screening sheet imported to production on 2026-10-12. Strong inference: it was not
  seeded data, which PRODUCT.md forbids. It is gone. All six product screenshots are now the current
  light workspace, captured from a synthetic screening day in a throwaway database (`nkwapa_shots`,
  dropped after capture): 12 invented patients spread along the station line, four staff on
  shifts with invented names, waits backdated so the line shows 2 to 27 minutes. The shared
  `nkwapa_audit` database was not used: its e2e leftovers ("Review Synthetic-8d4dbc9def", 11-hour
  waits, 252 drafts) would read as broken.
- **New file names** under `public/product/`. The image optimizer cached the old picture by URL and
  kept serving it after the file was replaced; a CDN would do the same.
- **What each frame shows.** Showcase: a manager's station line (`station-line.png`), with its four
  markers re-measured and re-labelled ("What needs you now", "Waits you can see"). Phone: a
  volunteer's Stations screen. Deck: a volunteer's Stations, a doctor's patient record (148/94
  mmHg from the BP station), a manager's dashboard. Journey: Review shows the doctor's record and
  Follow up the patient portal (`portal-phone.png`, cropped above an empty appointment block).
  Every alt text describes the new picture.
- **Craft floor.** The deck lost its "01 / 03" counters (not a sequence) and the gold role pill on
  every screenshot (it repeated the heading beside it). The journey's "01 / 05" stays: it is the
  visit's order. The laptop frame's chrome is the light canvas in both themes, matching the
  screenshots.
- **Motion.** Showcase markers no longer overshoot (`back.out(2.2)` from 0.6 scale): they ease in
  from 0.85 with `power3.out`, 80 ms apart. The scrubbed scenes were left alone: they follow the
  scroll, so they are already interruptible.
- **Legal pages, Read mode.** The notice now reads on the white sheet (`bg-card`) under the title on
  the canvas, the layering every workspace page uses. Below `lg` the 18-item contents list starts
  folded behind "Show all 18 sections" (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`; choosing a section folds
  it), so at 390px the notice starts around 770px down instead of about 1,800px. From `lg` up the
  sticky list is unchanged.
- **Dead CSS removed:** `.legal-prose` and `.legal-progress` in `globals.css` (nothing used them
  since #279).

## Decisions

- **Public pages stay full bleed; the inset sheet means "you are in Nkwapa".** Recorded in MASTER
  section 12 "Public site frame and product screenshots (R6.1)", with the screenshot recipe.
- Copy is unchanged apart from the showcase marker titles and texts. Every claim there is visible
  in the screenshot and is something the product does today. No counts, quotes or outcomes added.
- `og-image.png` (a photograph of the landing hero, 7 MB) was left: it shows no product screen. It
  predates the "Log in" button; carried to R7.1.

## Ranked findings left open

- **P2.** The marker nudge (markers 2 and 3 to 14.8%, marker 4 to 78.6%) was measured from the
  rendered 1440 showcase crop after the last build, but not re-rendered. Markers fade in on scroll,
  so full-page evidence does not show them.
- **P3.** A confirmed appointment inserted straight into the throwaway database did not appear in
  the portal's "Next appointment". Not traced; the screenshot avoided that block.
- **P3.** The deck and journey render the doctor's record twice on wide screens (Review step and
  deck card). They are different contexts and sizes; a separate doctor screenshot would need a
  finalized visit in the synthetic day.
