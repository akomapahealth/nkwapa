# R7.2 review: Keyboard and focus polish

**Scope:** frontend-only on `ui-refresh/r1-1-tokens`: keyboard and focus across the workspace,
portal and public pages, against MASTER section 10 and WCAG 2.2 AA (2.4.7 focus visible, 2.4.11
focus not obscured, 2.1.1 keyboard, 2.4.3 focus order). Local commit only; no push. No separate
agent review ran.

Guidance applied: Impeccable (context loader, audit framing, detector over the changed files: no
findings, regex pass only), Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 evidence-led workflow: find with a tool,
confirm by keyboard and eye), Emil Kowalski's design engineering (keyboard actions never animate),
Apple's fluid-interface guidance (feedback at the moment of input, nothing between the key and the
response). `web-design-guidelines` was loaded; its rules fetch was not run. Mode: Operate
(workspace, portal), Persuade (landing).

## How the findings were made

A scratch Playwright probe signed in as staff and patient, and anonymously, and walked 11 routes at
1440 and 390: `/dashboard`, `/stations`, `/queues`, `/patients`, a patient record,
`/patients/new`, `/portal`, `/portal/appointments`, `/portal/self-reports/new`, `/` and
`/privacy`. It pressed the skip link, then Tab until focus wrapped, and at every stop recorded
the indicator (outline or ring) at 0 ms and 250 ms. It also hit-tested a 5x5 grid inside the
element to see whether anything covered it. It flagged stops and screenshotted them, then opened
a Select by keyboard and ran axe's `aria-hidden-focus` rule. Two of its own metric bugs were fixed
first: Tailwind's `outline-none` is a 2px transparent outline, and covering needs a hit test, not
a rectangle overlap. The probe was deleted; `e2e/keyboard-focus.spec.js` keeps the cases.

### Why the inert mirror cannot strand focus

Radix's `FocusScope` returns focus on unmount inside `setTimeout(0)` (checked in the installed
`@radix-ui/react-focus-scope`). The `aria-hidden` package removes its marker during the same
unmount, and a MutationObserver callback is a microtask, so `inert` is cleared before focus moves.
Opening: FocusScope's mount effect moves focus into the popup before the parent's `hideOthers`
effect runs, so the trigger is never inert while focused. The new spec checks both directions on
a Select. Sheets returning focus (R7.1 regression in `sheet-drag`) and the open-Select sweep in
`accessibility.spec` passed.

### Specs adjusted for `inert`

Two sweeps ran full-page axe with a modal open. With the background inert, axe stops counting
what is behind the modal: the chart's `h1` (`page-has-heading-one`), and a sheet scroll region
whose children are hidden while the region stays (`scrollable-region-focusable`). Both sweeps now
cover the dialog in front (`include` by role or `aria-labelledby`). The background is swept in
its closed state by the same specs.

- `vitals-workspace.spec.js`: the Crisis alert sweep is scoped to `[role="alertdialog"]`.
- `responsive-components.spec.js`: the Discard confirmation sweep is scoped to that dialog.

## Keyboard routes

The probe walked the station line, queues, registry and record without a mouse; every walk wrapped
back to the skip link (no traps). With the fixes, every stop had a visible indicator and none was
covered. The vitals form is driven by Tab in `vitals.spec` and `vitals-workspace.spec` (both
passed). Visual check of focus in both themes, as screenshots: registry row action at 390 and 1440,
the bottom nav (inset ring), the sync sheet close button, and the landing header, release banner,
hero and CTA bands (white rings on photo and teal; dark ink on the bright dark-mode banner). The
sync sheet opened and closed by keyboard returned focus to its trigger in both themes.

## Ranked findings left open

- **P2.** Chromium gives a date field's calendar button its own tab stop, drawn with the browser's
  blue `auto` ring. A `::-webkit-calendar-picker-indicator:focus-visible` rule did not apply and
  was removed. The ring is visible (2.4.7 holds); only the colour differs. Carried to R7.3, which
  owns forms.
- **P3.** On phones, a section link chosen in the landing header sheet jumps rather than glides
  (the sheet sets `location.href` after it closes). Pointer clicks elsewhere glide.
- **P3.** While a modal is open, Sonner toasts and the cookie banner sit in the inert background.
  Radix already blocked outside pointer events, so this only adds keyboard and screen-reader
  consistency.
- **Not checked:** Safari and Firefox (`inert`, `:has()`, `scroll-padding` with focus), screen
  readers, real devices, the welcome tour by keyboard.
