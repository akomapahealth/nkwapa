# R7.3 review: Forms and modal refinements

**Scope:** frontend-only on `ui-refresh/r1-1-tokens`: forms and modals across the workspace, portal
and public pages, against MASTER sections 5.2, 5.4, 9.1 and 10. Validation rules, messages, clinical
saves, permissions and flags are unchanged. Local commit only; no push. No separate agent review
ran.

Guidance applied: Impeccable (context loader, polish playbook, craft floor, detector over the
changed files: no findings, regex pass only), Emil Kowalski's design engineering (transitions over
keyframes for anything that can be interrupted; keyboard actions never animate; modals scale from
their centre, not a trigger), Apple's fluid-interface guidance (interruptibility: a closing surface
starts from its on-screen value; feedback where the action is). Mode: Operate (workspace, portal).

## How the findings were made

A scratch Playwright probe (deleted) shot registration, clinic settings, the encounter and vitals
tab, the portal self-report and appointment request, and the Create clinic dialog at 390, 744 and
1440 in both themes, before and after a failed submit. A standalone Chromium page tested the date
field's calendar button. Two failures in the first run of the new dialog tests found the focus bug
below.

### The date-field decision

The native control stays. It brings the platform's picker on phones, locale order, typed entry and
screen-reader support; a house control would rebuild all of that to change one ring. The only defect
was the colour of that ring, which CSS now owns. Chromium only; Safari and Firefox have no calendar
tab stop.

### Sheets stay on keyframes

`use-sheet-drag` sets `transform` inline while dragging and the R7.1 note about
`transition-transform` still stands. Moving sheets to the transition clock would put the drag and
the clock on the same property. Their exit already reverses well enough at 190 ms; left as is.

## Checked and left alone

- Field rhythm on registration, vitals, the encounter and clinic settings at 390, 744 and 1440 in
  both themes: label 8px above the control, error directly below in one reserved line after a failed
  submit, sections separated by rules. Consistent; no change.
- Vitals already focused the first invalid field (`focusFirstInvalid`); registration already had
  the summary.

## Ranked findings left open

- **P2.** On phones, the long Create clinic dialog is a centred card that scrolls. MASTER 5.4
  prefers a bottom sheet or full-screen form there. A responsive-edge-case question for R7.4.
- **P3.** Vitals placeholders (120, 80, 72, 70, 170) are example values in muted ink; at a glance
  they can read as recorded data. Copy and clinical defaults are out of scope here.
- **P3.** The welcome tour still carries a small "Welcome tour" label above its progress bar.
- **Pre-existing, reproduced on HEAD without these changes:** `patient-redesign` expects a 44px tab
  where the R3.8 token gives 40px on a mouse; `vitals` cannot find the BMI text `24.2`. Both fail
  identically on a stashed HEAD build.
- **Not checked:** Safari and Firefox, screen readers, real devices, the triage and lifecycle
  dialogs' new focus in a browser (no request fixture; the code path is the one `focusField` tests
  cover elsewhere).
