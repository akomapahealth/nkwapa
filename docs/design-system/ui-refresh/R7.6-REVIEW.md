# R7.6 review: Dark-mode pass

**Scope:** frontend-only on `ui-refresh/r1-1-tokens`: dark mode across the workspace, portal and
public pages, against MASTER 4.3 and the commitment that light and dark are both designed and
measured. Clinical saves, validation, permissions, flags, sync and Keycloak are unchanged. Local
commit only; no push. No separate agent review ran.

Guidance applied: Impeccable (design hook over both changed files: no findings), accessibility
(WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 text, 3:1 icons, control boundaries and focus rings), Emil Kowalski's design
engineering and Apple's design principles for the switch (overlap old and new states instead of a
hard cut; a theme change is rare and user-initiated, so it may move, unlike state swaps).

## How the findings were made

1. **Every token pairing, computed.** A scratch script parsed the `.dark` block of `globals.css`
   and measured 270 pairings: ten text tokens and nine UI tokens on nine grounds (canvas, card,
   muted, selected, popover, mint, gold surface, deep, sidebar); every status ink on its opaque
   surface and on 10, 12, 15 and 22% tints over the card; foregrounds on their fills; chart series
   on card, canvas and muted; the eight avatar fills and inks; the sheet scrim. It reproduced the
   documented values (success ink 6.83:1, avatar initials 6.29 to 7.89:1), so its other numbers can
   be trusted.
2. **What the browser painted.** A scratch Chromium spec, dark from first paint, ran axe (WCAG 2.2
   AA tags) and took screenshots of the R7.5 states: the 404 `FullscreenStatus`, the chat staff
   picker's failed directory, the registry's offline notice, the patient record, and the landing
   hero (photographic band). All clean, and reviewed by eye.
3. **The six-role route audit** in both themes as regression.

### The switch

`switchTheme` in `lib/theme-context.tsx` wraps the class change in `document.startViewTransition`
when the user picks a theme (settings, account menu, landing toggle). Load stays on the boot script
(no fade, no flash) and following the OS stays instant. A pick that does not change the rendered
theme ("Match system" on a dark OS while dark) does not fade. Opacity only, so it also runs under
reduced motion, as dialogs do (MASTER 9.1). Browsers without View Transitions (Firefox before 144)
switch instantly. Measured: one transition per switch, `0.2s`, none on load, none on a same-theme
pick.

## Checked and left alone

- **Status surfaces** (success, warning, info, destructive, severe): inks 6.37 to 6.83:1 on their
  surfaces and 6.1:1 or better on every tint level used; foreground and ring clear them too.
- **Focus ring** `--ring`: 7.67:1 on card, 8.34:1 on canvas, 5.54:1 on selected, 3:1 or better on
  every status surface.
- **Control boundaries** `--input`: 4.27:1 on card, 4.64:1 on canvas.
- **Avatar tones:** initials 6.29 to 7.89:1. The fills sit close to the card (about 1.3:1); they are
  decoration on `aria-hidden` initials with the name beside them, so no 3:1 boundary is owed.
- **Chart series** on the card: 3.09 to 5.72:1. `--chart-3` is 2.87:1 on `--muted`, but both of its
  charts (patient trends single metric, portal weight) are single-series lines on the card. Raising
  it to 48% cleared muted but took chart-3 vs chart-5 (tritan) to deltaE 5.3, below the CI floor in
  `scripts/check-chart-palette.mjs`, so it stays at 46% with a comment. Rule: keep charts on the card.
- **Sheet scrim** `bg-black/80`: the dark card sits about 1.2:1 above it; dialogs and sheets carry a
  border, and controls inside measure 5.07:1 against the scrim.
- **Photographic band** (landing hero): its overlay is fixed dark in both themes; text and the
  white-ring buttons are unchanged by the theme.
- **Hard-coded colours:** none that ignore the theme. `bg-deep text-white` on the stations rail is
  dark teal in both themes; the app has no `dark:` utilities.
- **No flash on load:** the boot script in `app/layout.tsx` sets the class before paint;
  `dark-mode.spec.js` "survives a navigation without flashing light" passes.

## Ranked findings left open

- **P3.** The wordmark's teal (`nkwa`) reads dim on the dark canvas (404 sheet, sidebar). It is the
  logo, exempt from contrast; a dark-mode logo variant would be a brand decision.
- **P3.** The release banner on the landing page is a full-width bright teal band at the top of a
  dark page. Axe-clean; a brightness choice for the owner.
- **P3.** During the crossfade, controls with `transition-colors` also tween their own colours for
  150 ms inside the new snapshot. Not visible as a defect at 200 ms; not suppressed.
- **Not checked:** `PageSkeleton` in dark (whole-page loading is too brief to catch without
  throttling; it uses only `--muted` skeletons on the card), real Safari, real devices, screen
  readers.
