# Nkwapa 2.1.1 release notes

A patch release, `release:patch`, after v2.1.0 (2026-11-14), in release PR #371. It ships the
pilot-feedback features #331 to #352, the dashboard offline state (#366) and the security work
#361 to #370. Follow-up stratification (#353 to #357) and offline sessions (#358 to #360) ship
behind flags that stay off in production, so they are not announced here.

The release pull request's "For clinic teams" section lists the items below, then the 2.1 items
under their own headings. What's New shows only the latest release, so the 2.1 notes are carried;
the 2.0.1 and 2.0 notes are no longer carried and stay in `docs/releases/2.0.md` and the GitHub
releases. `FALLBACK_RELEASE` in `apps/web/lib/release-notes.ts` holds the same list.

## For clinic teams

### New

- **Volunteers sign a confidentiality agreement.** Before using Nkwapa, a volunteer reads nine short points, ticks a box and types their name to sign. Existing volunteers are asked the next time they open the app. Your account page shows when you signed, with a link to read it again.
- **A notice when your role changes.** When an administrator gives you a new role or removes one, Nkwapa tells you what changed. If you have no access left, it says so.
- **Search and filter a queue.** Find a patient in a queue by name or patient code, even if they are not on the first page. Filter by blood pressure class, high glucose or the dates a visit was created.
- **The patient at a glance.** The chart Overview opens with the patient's conditions, latest blood pressure and glucose with their direction, medications and adherence, and risks such as allergies and tobacco.
- **Full middle names.** Patients and staff can have a full middle name, not just an initial. Existing initials are kept.
- **Where a patient lives.** Record a patient's Digital Address, a landmark and their home's position on a map.
- **A password-protected referral report.** Doctors, volunteers and managers can make a PDF referral report for a patient, choose what goes in it, and download or email it. The PDF opens with a 16-character passcode. A patient's referral consent is recorded on the Consent tab, separately from research consent.
- **A welcome tour for each role.** A short tour shows each role where its work lives. On a phone it appears as a centred dialog.
- **A second sign-in step.** In Settings, staff can add an authenticator app or a passkey, so signing in asks for it after the password. System administrators must set one up.

### Improved

- **The dashboard when you are offline.** Instead of an error, the dashboard says its figures need a connection, shows what still works offline, and keeps any figures already on screen with the time they were loaded.

### Fixed

- **Research exports open safely in spreadsheets.** A value that starts like a spreadsheet formula is now exported as plain text.
